About me

Hey, I'm Zenthrox Orinthal — a developer, computer science student at the University of Ghana, and a builder at heart.

My journey into tech started with a simple question: "How does this actually work?" That curiosity never left. Six years later, it's still what drives me — whether I'm untangling a tricky bug, designing a clean interface, or learning something completely new just because I want to understand it.

I'm currently studying Computer Science at the University of Ghana, but my experience goes well beyond the classroom. Over the past six years, I've built and shipped real projects, led teams, and worked with clients who needed things done right.

What I've Been Up To

  • Head of Web Development at Stusil — leading the web team, setting direction, and making sure we deliver quality work.
  • Frontend Developer at Vcrypt Finance — building responsive, user-focused interfaces for a fintech product.
  • Self Portfolio — designed and developed my own portfolio from scratch (you're looking at it).

What I Do

I specialize in frontend development and web technologies, but my curiosity keeps me exploring across the stack. I care about clean code, thoughtful design, and building things that actually solve problems.
